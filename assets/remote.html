Pair this browser

Scan the machine's pairing QR or paste its JSON. Pair only with a relay you trust. The private key stays saved in this browser until you forget it. Bearer tokens are kept only in memory.

Anyone with access to this browser's storage can use the saved key. Do not pair on a shared device.

Transfer key to another device

Open this relay's bhai website on the other phone and scan this code to copy the controller key. Anyone who scans it gets full control of your sessions. Show it only to a device you trust, then hide it.

Can't scan? Copy the pairing data instead

Paste this into the other browser's pairing box. Treat it like a password.
